1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Asia Starmar Transport Intelligent Co., Ltd. ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares, stores and protects personal data when you use the YPS + mobile application (the "App") and the transport-card and payment services available through it.

The App is used to apply for and manage a transport card, check a card balance, top up a card, pay by QR code, view transaction records, and complete the identity verification that applicable regulations require for these services.

This policy applies to the App and to the account services connected to it. It does not apply to third-party websites, applications or services you may reach from the App, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

This policy is published at https://www.ypsmm.com/privacy-site/index.html and is also accessible from within the App. It is provided in English, Chinese and Myanmar. In the event of any conflict between language versions, the English version prevails; translations are provided for convenience only.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide to us

  • Account information: your mobile phone number and password (your password is stored in a protected, non-plaintext form), and your name.
  • Identity verification information: the identity document information (for example national ID or passport number and details) and photographs of your identity document that you submit when you complete real-name verification. This information is required by applicable law for transport-card and payment services. It is sensitive personal information and we handle it with additional care.
  • Photographs you choose to submit, for example identity-document photos or attachments you send with feedback.
  • Communications with us: the content of emails or messages you send to our customer service, including any information you choose to include.

2.2 Information collected automatically when you use the App

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version and language settings, used to keep the App compatible with your device and to diagnose problems.
  • Service and transaction records: card number, card balance, top-up and payment records, transaction history, request logs, and the IP address used to connect to our services.
  • Network status: whether your device is online, so that the App can handle connectivity failures correctly.

2.3 Information we receive from third parties

  • Google Sign-In: if you choose to sign in with Google, we receive a Google account identifier, your email address and your profile picture in order to create or link your account with us. Google's handling of your data is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

2.4 Information we do not collect

We do not collect or access the following:

  • Your location. The App does not request location permission. The outlet map inside the App only displays our network of service outlets and does not read or use your device location.
  • Your photo or video library. The App does not request permission to read your media library. When you are asked to choose a photo, the App uses your device's system photo picker, which gives the App access only to the specific images you select.
  • Your SMS messages, call logs, contacts or calendar. We do not read your messages. Verification codes you receive are used only to complete the action you requested. (The App reads and writes data on a transport card using NFC when you tap the card — see Section 4.)
  • Advertising identifiers or device identifier SDKs. The App does not include advertising SDKs and does not collect advertising identifiers or device-identifier (such as OAID/AAID) data.

3. How we use your information

We use the information described above only for the following purposes:

  • Creating and securing your account, including authentication and sign-in (including sign-in with Google if you choose it).
  • Providing the services you request: card application and management, balance enquiry, top-up, QR payment, and transaction records.
  • Complying with legal obligations: real-name verification, anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorism-financing checks, and keeping transaction records for the period required by law.
  • Customer support: responding to your questions, requests and complaints.
  • Safety and security: detecting, preventing and investigating fraud, abuse, unauthorised access and other misuse, and protecting the integrity of our services.
  • Operating and improving the App: diagnosing technical faults, ensuring the App works correctly, and improving reliability and usability.
  • Service communications: sending you service notices that relate directly to your account, card or transactions.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your personal data (including Google user data) for advertising or for building advertising profiles, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. We only share personal data as described in Section 5, or where required by law.

6. Storage, retention and deletion of data

Where your data is stored. Your personal data is stored on servers operated for us and located in Yangon, Myanmar, and is processed by our staff and service providers who need it to provide the services described in this policy.

What happens when you delete your account.

  1. Your account is closed immediately and you can no longer sign in to it. Closing an account cannot be undone.
  2. Your account and the data associated with it — including your account record (profile information such as your nickname and avatar), your identity-verification information, your card, transaction and settlement records, and related service records — is retained for 2 years from the date you delete your account. This period applies because applicable financial and anti-money-laundering regulations require these records to be kept, and because we need them for security and fraud-prevention purposes.
  3. During that period the retained data is used only for those purposes (legal compliance and audit, security, and fraud prevention). It is not used for marketing or for any other purpose, and access to it is restricted to staff who need it.
  4. When the 2-year retention period ends, we delete the data or irreversibly anonymise it.
  5. If you register again later, we create a new account for you. The retained records described above are not linked to your new account, and your previous account cannot be restored.

We disclose this retention practice here because the legal, security and fraud-prevention requirements described above mean that this data cannot be removed immediately when an account is closed. See Section 11 and our Account & Data Deletion page for how to request deletion.

For the detailed steps to delete your account and data, see Section 11 and our Account & Data Deletion page.

7. International data transfers

Our services are operated in Yangon, Myanmar, and your personal data is stored in Myanmar. We do not transfer your personal data to other countries on our own initiative. Some information may nevertheless be processed outside Myanmar by the third-party services we use to provide the App's features — such as Google Sign-In and Google Maps, which are provided by Google and may process data on servers in other countries. Where that happens, we take steps to ensure your data continues to be protected, including by contractual and technical safeguards. Details of the locations involved and of the safeguards applied: the recipients are the service providers listed in Section 5, and the safeguards applied are those providers' own data-protection terms together with encryption in transit.

Where the App uses Google services (Sign-In and Maps), Google may process data in countries other than yours, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy.

8. How we protect your information

  • Data transmitted between the App and our servers is protected in transit (encrypted connections).

  • We monitor our services for abuse and unauthorised access, and we review our practices as regulations and technology change.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please keep your password confidential, and contact us promptly at astimyanmar@gmail.com if you believe your account has been accessed without your permission.

6. 数据存储、保留与删除

数据存储位置。您的个人数据存储在我们位于缅甸仰光的服务器上，并由为提供本政策所述服务而确需接触这些数据的我方人员与服务商处理。

注销账号后会发生什么。

  1. 您的账号将立即关闭，之后无法再登录；账号关闭不可撤销。
  2. 您的账号及与其关联的数据——包括账号记录（昵称、头像等资料信息）、实名认证信息、卡片、交易与结算记录及相关服务记录——将自您注销之日起保留 2 年。保留的原因：适用的金融与反洗钱法规要求保存这些记录，同时我们基于安全与反欺诈目的需要保留。
  3. 保留期间，这些数据仅用于上述目的（合规审计、安全与反欺诈），不用于营销或任何其他用途；访问权限仅限于确需接触的人员。
  4. 2 年保留期届满后，我们将删除这些数据或进行不可逆的匿名化处理。
  5. 若您之后重新注册，我们将为您创建全新账号；上述保留的记录不会与新账号关联，原账号也无法恢复。

我们在此明确告知该保留做法：基于上述法律、安全与反欺诈要求，这些数据在账号关闭时无法立即删除。注销账号的具体操作方式见第 11 节及《账号与数据删除》页面。

我们的服务运营地为缅甸仰光，您的个人数据存储于缅甸境内。我们不会主动将您的个人数据传输至其他国家/地区。但为实现应用功能而使用的第三方服务（例如由 Google 提供的 Google 登录与 Google 地图）可能会在其位于其他国家的服务器上处理部分信息。若发生此类传输，我们将采取合同与技术层面的保护措施，确保您的数据持续受到保护。涉及的具体地区与保护措施：接收方为本政策第 5 节所列的服务商，所采取的保护措施为该等服务商自身的数据保护条款以及传输加密。
