**Privacy Policy**

The YPS Plus team (hereinafter referred to as "We") places great importance on user privacy and the protection of personal information. When you use our application, we may collect and use your relevant information. We are fully aware of the importance of personal information to you and will make every effort to protect your personal information, ensuring it is safe and reliable. We are committed to maintaining your trust in us and will adhere to the following principles to safeguard your personal information.

Therefore, we hereby remind you to carefully read and fully understand our Privacy Policy (hereinafter referred to as "this Policy"). This Policy applies to the products or services provided by us through the platform application.

If you are fully aware of and understand its contents and are deemed to have accepted this Policy by checking and clicking "Agree." If you do not agree with this Policy, you should immediately cease using our products/services.

**1. Purpose of Personal Information Collection**

To provide our services through this application, we need to collect personal information from you when you use it. We collect your information for the following purposes:

• Core business functions (e.g., the NRC registration function will request your information so you can publish your creations)

• Additional business functions, such as profile information

• Payment functions for the various services operated by the application

• To prevent misuse of the various services operated by the application

**2. Proper Collection of Personal Information**

We collect personal information in an appropriate manner and do not collect personal information through deception or other fraudulent means. When we collect personal information, we will notify or announce the purpose of use in advance, except in the cases specified in Section 3 below.

We will not handle personal information beyond the scope reasonably recognized as relevant to the purpose of use. If there is a change in the purpose of use of personal information, we will notify the individual of the changes before implementing them.

**3. Exceptions to Obtaining Consent**

In the following situations, we may obtain relevant information without seeking your prior consent:

• Directly related to national security and defense security

• Directly related to public security, public health, and significant public interests

• Directly related to criminal investigation, prosecution, trial, and execution of judgments

• When it is difficult to obtain your consent in a timely manner for the purpose of safeguarding your or others' life, property, or other major legal rights and interests

• The collected personal information is disclosed to the public by yourself

• Necessary for signing and performing the contract at your request

• Your personal information is collected from legally disclosed information, such as legal news reports, government information disclosure, and other channels

• Necessary to maintain the safe and stable operation of the products or services provided, such as discovering and handling product or service failures

• Other circumstances stipulated by laws and regulations

**4. Correction of Personal Information**

In the application, you can log in to your account at any time and access your personal information. When you find that the personal information about you is incorrect, you can correct it immediately via the application. If you cannot correct the personal information through the application, you have the right to request us to make corrections via customer service. We will promptly conduct the necessary investigation and take appropriate action. Once we have made the correction, we will notify the individual accordingly.

**5. Deletion of Personal Information**

You may request us to delete your personal information under the following circumstances:

• If our handling of personal information violates laws and regulations

• If we collect and use your personal information without your consent

• If our handling of personal information violates our agreement with you

• If you no longer use our products or services, or if you cancel your account

• If we no longer provide products or services to you

If we decide to respond to your deletion request, we will also notify the entities that obtained your personal information from us and request them to delete it in a timely manner, unless otherwise provided by laws and regulations, or these entities have obtained your independent authorization. After you delete information from our service, we may not immediately delete the corresponding information from the backup system but will delete it when the backup is updated.

**6. Outsourcing**

When we outsource to external parties the implementation of all or part of the application that may require personal information, we will sign strict confidentiality agreements with them regarding the processing of personal information. We ensure that the outsourced party complies with this Policy.

**7. Sharing**

We will not share your personal information with any company, organization, or individual other than our company, except in the following cases:

• Shared with your express consent

• Shared externally in accordance with laws and regulations or the mandatory requirements of government authorities

• Shared with authorized partners

**8. Public Disclosure**

We will only publicly disclose your personal information under the following circumstances:

• After obtaining your explicit consent

• We may publicly disclose your personal information when required by laws, legal procedures, litigation, or government authorities.

However, if the disclosure falls under any of the following, we may not disclose all or part of it, and if we decide not to disclose it, we will notify you without delay:

• When there is a risk of harming the life, body, property, or other rights and interests of the individual or a third party

• When there is a risk of significantly hindering the proper implementation of our business

• When it violates other laws and regulations

**9. Data Security**

• We will endeavor to keep personal information accurate and up-to-date for its intended purpose of use. Additionally, we have adopted industry-standard security measures to protect the personal information you provide, preventing unauthorized access, public disclosure, use, modification, damage, or loss of data. We will take all reasonable and feasible measures to protect your personal information. For example, we will use encryption technology to ensure the confidentiality of data and trusted protection mechanisms to prevent data from malicious attacks.

• The internet environment is not 100% secure, and we will do our best to ensure or guarantee the security of any information you send to us.

• In the unfortunate event of a personal information security incident, we will promptly inform you in accordance with legal and regulatory requirements. When it is difficult to notify each individual subject of personal information, we will issue an announcement in a reasonable and effective manner. At the same time, we will actively report the handling of personal information security incidents as required by regulatory authorities.

**10. Personal Information of Minors**

Our application is primarily intended for adults. Children should not create their own accounts using their personal information without the consent of their parents or guardians.
